# PopReel Privacy Policy

_Effective date: 23 November 2026_

This policy explains what information the PopReel Android app ("PopReel", "the app") handles and why. PopReel is published by eMax Software ("we", "us").

## Summary

- PopReel has **no accounts** and does **not ask for your name, email, location, contacts or any other personal information**.
- Your movie choices, settings and game scores are **stored on your device**. We do not receive them.
- PopReel contains **no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services**, and **does not sell or share** your information.
- To show movies, the app downloads a movie catalog and poster images from the internet. Like any internet request, those servers can see your device's IP address.

## Information stored on your device

PopReel keeps the following only in the app's private storage on your device:

| Information | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Your movie choices (liked, disliked, not seen) and when you made them | Show your choices, keep marked films out of Swipe and Genie, and personalise Genie recommendations |
| App settings (appearance and game sound) | Remember your preferences |
| Arcade statistics (trivia points, best score, best streak, rounds played, puzzles solved) | Show your progress in the Arcade |
| A copy of the public movie catalog and trivia questions | Let you browse and play offline |

Genie recommendations are calculated **on your device** from your choices. Your choices are never sent to us or to anyone else.

You can change or clear any movie choice in the app, from a movie's details or from "Your choices" in Swipe. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android Settings, deletes all of this information from your device.

### Android backup

Android can include app data in your device backup to your Google account (Android Auto Backup), and when you move to a new phone. If you have backup turned on, your choices, settings and Arcade statistics may be included in that backup. Google handles that backup under its own privacy policy, and you control it in your device's Settings. We cannot access your backups.

## Network requests

PopReel connects to the internet to:

- **Download the movie catalog and trivia** from the PopReel catalog service at `movie-api-delta-six.vercel.app`, hosted by Vercel.
- **Load poster images** from The Movie Database image service (`image.tmdb.org`) and Wikimedia (`upload.wikimedia.org`, `thumb.wikimedia.org`).

These requests contain only what is needed to fetch the content: the address of the content, standard technical information such as your IP address, and an app identifier of the form "PopReel/1.0 (Android; com.emaxsoftware.popreel)" that names the app and its version. They do **not** include your choices, settings or any identifier we assign to you. The operators of these services may keep standard server logs under their own privacy policies:

- Vercel: https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy
- The Movie Database (TMDB): https://www.themoviedb.org/privacy-policy
- Wikimedia Foundation: https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Privacy_policy

## Links and sharing

- **View source:** a movie's "source" link opens the original record (for example on Wikipedia or Kaggle) in your browser. That website's own privacy policy applies.
- **Share:** when you share a movie, PopReel hands the movie's title and source link to the app you choose. We do not see what you share or with whom.

## Permissions

PopReel requests only the **Internet** permission, to download the catalog and images. It does not access your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or files.

## Children

PopReel is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. The catalog includes films of every rating, including horror and adult-oriented titles, along with their posters.

## Your rights

Because we do not collect or store your personal information, we hold no data about you to access, correct or delete. Everything PopReel stores is on your device, where you can change it in the app or delete it by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling. If you have questions about your rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA, contact us using the details below.

## Changes to this policy

If PopReel starts handling information differently, we will update this policy and change the effective date above before the change takes effect.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at https://github.com/shimjudavid/PopReel-Movie-Android-App/issues. Issues are public, so please don't include personal details you want kept private.
